Wake Up!: Stereo sounds-based Game

Course: [GCT535] Sound Technology for Multimedia (KAIST)
Role: Project Leader & Developer
Stack: C++, Unity, Visual Rendering
📄 Report 🖥️ Presentation 🚀 Download Game (.zip)

Project Description

The goal of this game is 'Wake up the family and turn off the alarm'. The player has to assist family members in waking up on time for their daily routines such as work or school, by finding them and gathering necessary items based on the stereo sound effects following the schedule. The game is played in the third-person view.
