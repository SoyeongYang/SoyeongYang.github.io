[ISMAR2025] AquaHaptics

Demo at International Symposium on Mixed and Augmented Reality (ISMAR) 2025

Abstract PDF Demo Video Poster

Project Description

This demo presents AquaHaptics, a hand-based multimodal haptic interaction system designed for immersive virtual underwater experiences. The system integrates pressure, vibration, and thermal feedback to simulate the complex physical sensations of being underwater.

Key Technologies

Using Unity and custom-built physical computing modules (Arduino), we developed a rendering algorithm that ensures cognitively meaningful haptic feedback during VR underwater navigation.
